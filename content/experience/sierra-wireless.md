On the test engineering team at Sierra Wireless, I focused on modem reliability, regression testing, and hardware-software integration for cellular IoT products.

I designed and executed regression and stress tests for modem firmware, translating manual test cases into automated Python and Selenium tests built on internal libraries. I deployed the automated tests on hardware test racks and developed SMS and hardware testing workflows that reduced testing time and costs by approximately 75%.

I also triaged bug reports and customer-reported issues, collaborating with firmware and hardware engineering teams to drive defects from initial discovery through resolution. This involved developing reproducible test cases to isolate issues, validate fixes, and prevent regressions. In addition, I performed specialized hardware and driver testing with the Windows Hardware Lab Kit (HLK) and prepared test reports documenting the results and findings.

Alongside my technical responsibilities, I helped onboard and train incoming co-op students by developing training plans, delegating learning tasks, and reviewing their progress in regular one-on-one meetings.

At Sierra Wireless, I learned how systematic testing can make hardware and software work together more reliably. I also enjoyed helping new co-op students get comfortable with the team and the work, alongside my day-to-day testing responsibilities.
