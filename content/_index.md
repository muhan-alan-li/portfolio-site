Hi, I'm **Muhan**. I'm a software engineer based in Vancouver, BC. I recently graduated from UBC with a degree in Computer Engineering.

My first computer was the family PC that ran windows xp. Then in seventh grade I started torrenting games on my first macbook. Fifteen years of my most formative years on a computer has helped me build an intuition for intentional and quality user experience.

Across three internships, I owned the development of end to end features for enterprise, academic, and and consumer users. At my most recent internship with Apera, I focused on building systems that produced reliable and repeatable outcomes without losing sight of the user experience.

I believe good software is intentional. For me, that means writing software that remains understandable, modular, and reliable as it grows. As I grow as an engineer, I want to build the skillset that better enables me to do so.
